# UI simplification pass (2026-10-04)

Prompt: "too much business on the screen." Evidence: the owner's connection-list screenshot and before/after captures of the real app at 1280x800 and 412x915 (local, ignored: `webApp/generated/ui-simplify-20261004/before` and `after`, produced by `shots.mjs`, which drives first visit, planner, chooser, route preview, a preview with an interior connection, active navigation, a paused interruption, a signal loss, and Saved).

What was crowding the screen: a large "Route preview" heading plus an eyebrow, a machine-written route sentence repeating the numbers just above it, a three-sentence connection box, a closure line, a full-width legend, a large orange block for the foreground-only warning, Start, a second foreground-only caption below six equal buttons, and on the ride screen the same pause explanation three times (an opening caption, a wake-lock line, and a closing paragraph), with Start (preview) and Stop (ride) below the fold on a phone.

What changed (copy and layout only; no routing, closure, Start-gate, estimated-connection or data change):
- Preview: eyebrow removed; heading and route stats smaller; the "Trail route found..." sentence kept for screen readers only; the connection box is two short lines (count, total length, "not confirmed by map data", then the numbered rows, so the genuine 37 ft "S Hershey Rd to trail" step is still announced and reachable); the legend is smaller and quieter; the foreground-only warning is one compact left-ruled note (same `#foreground-note`, still read with Start); the repeated closing caption is removed; the six secondary buttons are one compact row of quiet buttons; the closure status and every closure notice, recalculation offer, estimated-connection caption and private test-mode note remain.
- Phone: the map is a fixed 40% of the screen and Start is a sticky bar at the bottom of the screen, so the primary action is reachable with the route, stats and connection notice in view.
- Ride: eyebrow removed; one caption replaces the three pause explanations; the wake-lock line shows only when the lock is not working; everything else (guidance card, directions, direction control, reroute choices, Stop) is unchanged.
- Copy updated only where tests pinned the old wording (`help.spec.ts` now expects "Keep this page open and visible. Guidance pauses").

Checked: tsc; unit 579 of 580 (1 skipped); full default browser suite 280 passed (4 skipped) and county-mode suite 103 passed on desktop and phone Chromium, including the existing axe sweeps; new `simplified-layout.spec.ts` (Start inside the first screen, map share, each note said once, secondary actions present, axe on preview and ride, an interruption and recovery, and a second ride started after stopping the first) passes. The existing interior-connection and test-mode specs pass unchanged.

Limits: WebKit is not installed here, so the WebKit specs were not run locally (the changed wording is kept where they assert it: the foreground note, the ride pause sentence, "observed this ride"). Layout judgments were made from captures at two sizes in Chromium, not on devices. Screenshots are local review aids and are not committed.

## Follow-up: why Start is blocked, and what is disclosed

- A blocked Start now says why right under the button, inside the sticky bar on phones: `src/startReason.ts` returns one short line (stale data, a closure, "N estimated connections (start/end included) are not confirmed by map data", a generic block, or offline) and never decides whether Start is allowed. The button's `aria-describedby` names the foreground note and the reason, and the fuller caption stays in the page.
- Save, Share, Directions and Recalculate stay visible; Edit and "Save destination as a place" are under one "More actions" disclosure, and the map key is under "Map key" in the preview (Explore and the map picker still show it open). Twelve test call sites were updated to open the disclosure first.
- Checked: unit 583 of 584 (1 skipped); default browser suite 287 passed, 4 skipped, 1 mobile test (`image goes to the share sheet`) failed once and passed twice on rerun (a timing flake in a share-sheet test, not related to this change); county-mode suite 103; test-mode specs 12 (6 skipped); new reason spec cases (blocked reason adjacent to Start and described by it, offline reason and recovery). Final captures: `webApp/generated/ui-simplify-20261004/final`. In full-page captures of the phone layout the sticky bar appears mid-page; that is a capture artifact.
