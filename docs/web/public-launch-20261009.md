Jesse authorized public launch of the existing reviewed composition and live
foreground guidance on October 9, 2026 at 21:05 UTC. He chose public feedback to
inform further physical testing rather than holding publication for those runs.

license. Physical iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and outdoor GPS acceptance remain

`release/dataset.county.json` records his composition approval against the exact
existing network, source manifests, access base/index, supplement and absence of

and 9,344 OSM service roads in 440 tiles. No data is added or removed. Existing
source terms, attribution and complete data/exact construction-source links remain.

an extra permission requirement to the included licensed county subset.

Use `cd webApp && npm run build:public`. This refuses a fixture, review channel,
private estimated-connection override or dirty source, then builds with public
channel and the override disabled, writes strict provenance and recomputes public
eligibility against the committed composition and every artifact file. It does
not deploy or change sharing. The existing core's closure/proposed/estimated-gap
checks remain intact. Supported routes can guide; estimated plans can still be
reviewed/saved/exported but cannot start or resume guidance. A private ride left
in the browser must be reinspected under the strict gate.

There is no site-wide beta label, banner or new generic phone-testing warning.
Only active turn-by-turn guidance shows the owner's requested note:
"Navigation is in beta and may experience issues." It clears during acquisition,
pause/loss and after Stop. Specific closure, access and foreground safety notices
and visible map attribution remain; removed private cards stay removed.

Validation includes the real router with synthetic supported/gap plans, an old
private active-ride record, fresh simulated location, acquisition/pause/loss/Stop,
small and enlarged text, existing noisy/stale/jump/reroute replay controls,
